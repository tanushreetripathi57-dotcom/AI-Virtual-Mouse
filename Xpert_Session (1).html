📏 Euclidean Distance: Measuring in Space

Distance = √[(x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²]

🤔 What is Euclidean Distance?

It's the straight-line distance between two points - like drawing a line between your thumb and index finger!

Distance = √[(x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²]

Real-world example: Distance between your house and school "as the crow flies"

In our code: How far apart are your thumb and index finger?

def get_distance(landmark_list): (x1, y1), (x2, y2) = landmark_list[0], landmark_list[1] L = np.hypot(x2 - x1, y2 - y1) # This calculates Euclidean distance! return np.interp(L, [0, 1], [0, 1000])

def move_mouse(index_finger_tip): if index_finger_tip is not None: x = int(index_finger_tip.x * screen_width) y = int(index_finger_tip.y / 2 * screen_height) # Note the /2 ! pyautogui.moveTo(x, y)

🔄 Coordinate Magic Explained

MediaPipe Says

x = 0.5, y = 0.3

(Values between 0 and 1)

We Convert To

x = 960, y = 324

(Actual pixel positions)

The mysterious "/2": We only use the top half of your camera view!

Why? So you don't have to stretch your arm to reach the bottom of the screen!

📐 It's like converting from "percentage of the room" to "exact steps from the corner"
